What this data measures: The Indeed adclick2 index records every job click event — both sponsored and organic listings. Each row represents one click by a job seeker. This analysis filters to clicks where the search query contained a maternity-related keyword (e.g. "maternity care assistant", "maternity cover", "maternity nurse"). The sector shown is based on the job that was clicked, not the search intent. So a candidate searching "maternity cover" who clicks a Care sector role is counted in Care. This reveals which sectors are successfully capturing candidate attention when maternity benefits are front of mind — and where Virtue's announcement will land with the highest-intent audience.

04 — Strategic Insights
Turning Search Behaviour into Hiring Advantage
Four board-level findings from the keyword data — translated into actionable direction for Virtue's Sponsored Jobs and Employer Branding strategy.
Insight 01 — Care Sector Dominance
51.5%
Care Owns the Majority of All Maternity-Related Job Clicks in Ireland
With 1,036 of 2,011 total clicks, the Care sector is the clear #1. "Maternity care assistant" alone drove 384 searches — the single highest-volume individual term in the entire dataset. Virtue's announcement is precisely on-brand for this audience. Sponsored Jobs targeting this keyword should see strong click-through and direct pipeline impact in Care Homes and Home Care divisions alike.
Insight 02 — Cross-Sector Cover Demand
5 of 6
"Maternity Cover" Appears in 5 of 6 Sectors — Signalling Temp Role Demand
This term surfaces across Care (163 clicks), Customer Service (137), Food (19), Childcare (11), and Personal Care (2). It signals candidates actively seeking temporary placements to fill roles during colleagues' maternity leave — a consistent gap Virtue can advertise against. Highlighting flexible and fixed-term contract options alongside the enhanced benefit in job copy positions Virtue ahead of employers leading only on salary.
Insight 03 — Nursing Differentiation
532
Med/Nurse at 532 Clicks — Care Home Roles Must Lead with Benefits
With "maternity nurse" generating 234 clicks, this is an active direct clinical role search. Virtue's Care Homes (Caroline, Márcio) should feature the enhanced maternity benefit prominently in all RGN and HCA job descriptions to differentiate from competitors who have not yet made this move. Ireland's first private nursing home group to offer a paid top-up is a genuine, defensible differentiator at the point of candidate decision.
Insight 04 — Budget Concentration
1.7%
Childcare and Personal Care Are Negligible — Focus Spend Where It Counts
Combined, Childcare (29 clicks) and Personal Care (6 clicks) represent just 1.7% of all maternity-related job clicks. Virtue's keyword targeting budget should be concentrated entirely on Care and Med/Nurse sectors. Geo-targeting should focus on Fingal and Dublin City — the two highest-demand Local Authority Areas in Virtue's existing market intelligence data from the Dublin Healthcare Report.
Recommended Action — Employer Branding & Sponsored Jobs
Lead with Ireland's First Paid Maternity Top-Up in Private Nursing
Position Virtue as: "The first private nursing home group in Ireland to offer a paid maternity top-up." Use "maternity care assistant", "maternity cover", and "maternity support worker" as primary keyword targets in Sponsored Jobs and Employer Branding Ads across Virtue's business units. Geo-target campaigns to Fingal and Dublin City — the two highest-demand Local Authority Areas in Virtue's existing Dublin Healthcare Market Intelligence data. This announcement is a genuine differentiator that no competitor has matched yet — it should be placed front and centre in all job copy across Home Care and Care Homes.
